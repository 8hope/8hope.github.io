Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy covers the "Personal rclone backup" Google OAuth client described on the home page.

What data is accessed

The app accesses only the Google Drive of the one person who operates it, and only after that person signs in and grants access. With the drive.file scope it sees only the files it uploaded itself. The drive scope is used only by the owner, from their own computer, to manage their own files.

Where data goes

Data moves only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. Backup files are encrypted on the computer before upload. The OAuth tokens are stored on that computer in an encrypted rclone configuration file. No data is sent to the author of this page or to any third party, and there is no server operated for this app.

Sharing and selling

No data is shared, sold, or used for advertising or profiling. Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the backup, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Backup files stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops the app from reaching Drive.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/8hope/8hope.github.io.
